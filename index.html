About SCHOLAR

SCHOLAR is a single place to discover scholarships, entrance exams, internships, admissions, certifications and career resources — mostly focused on India — without digging through dozens of scattered government and institutional websites.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND HERE

A growing, hand-indexed directory of scholarships, exams, internships, admissions and certifications, each linking straight to its official source — never a blog, news article or reseller.

PERSONALIZED FOR YOU

Once you're signed in, fill in your field of interest, education level, career goal and location preference in your profile, and SCHOLAR ranks the directory and builds a step-by-step Goal Map around it — transparently, based on matching keywords, with no black-box model.

ALWAYS VERIFY OFFICIALLY

Deadlines, amounts and eligibility rules are set by the issuing bodies and can change each cycle — SCHOLAR helps you find the right official page, but the final source of truth is always that page.
